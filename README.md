# StudyForge AI

AI-powered study assistant for students.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://www.studyforge.sbs/

StudyForge AI is a full-stack AI study platform designed to help students learn, revise, and practice in one place.

## Features

- 🤖 AI Tutor Chat
- 📝 AI Quiz Generator
- 🃏 AI Flashcard Generator
- 📄 AI PDF Study Tool
- 🗺️ Study Roadmaps
- 🔥 Study Streaks
- 🎭 AI Personas

## Study Tools

### AI Study Assistant
Get help understanding topics and concepts with an AI tutor.

### AI Quiz Generator
Create practice quizzes to test your knowledge.

### AI Flashcard Generator
Generate flashcards for revision and active recall.

### AI PDF Study Tool
Study your own PDF notes and learning material with AI.
